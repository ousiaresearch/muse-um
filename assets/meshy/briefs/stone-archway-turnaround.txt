Use case: 3D reconstruction reference sheet. The two views get fed to a multi-image
reconstruction service, so they must be the SAME OBJECT seen from two angles, drawn in one image.

Asset type: an object turnaround sheet — two views of one stone doorway surround, in a single row.

Style rules: match the attached reference exactly in material language and atmosphere. This is the
MUSE-UM pavilion idiom: hand-drawn European graphic-novel rendering, medieval epic and ancient sacred
architecture, ethereal, a forgotten civilization remembering itself beneath a dying sun. Dark
hand-cut stone with chisel marks and worn edges, thin engraved linework, muted bone and warm umber
highlights. Matte hand-finished surfaces, no plastic sheen, no chrome, no neon, no gloss.

The object: a freestanding doorway surround carved from dark hand-cut stone, standing upright on its
own. Two thick rectangular jambs stand vertically, and a semicircular arch of separate wedge-shaped
voussoir stones spans between them, with a slightly larger and more prominent keystone at the top
centre of the arch. A shallow band of simple carved chevrons runs along the face of the arch stones.
A low threshold step of the same stone sits at the base between the jambs. The stone is thick —
clearly much deeper front-to-back than a thin panel — with the arch and jambs reading as solid blocks
of masonry with worn, chipped edges.

The door opening in the middle is a TRUE THROUGH-HOLE: the background must be visible straight
through it, with no door, no panel, no glazing, and nothing filling the opening. The hole should be a
clean unambiguous void with a clear silhouette.

Keep the ornament BOLD AND SIMPLE — broad readable shapes, not fine filigree. Fine detail smears when
the image is reconstructed into geometry.

Views, left to right, all the same object at the same scale and the same height in frame:
  1. front elevation, looking straight through the opening
  2. three-quarter view, turned roughly forty degrees so the thickness of the masonry and the depth
     of the opening are clearly visible

Composition: the two views evenly spaced across one horizontal sheet, separated by a clear empty gap
so they can be cut apart. Identical scale and identical lighting in both views. Each view upright,
fully inside the frame with generous empty margin on all sides. No overlap, no touching edges.

Use the LARGEST canvas you can produce, and make the object as large within the frame as the margins
allow — every extra pixel of the object survives into the reconstruction.

Background: perfectly uniform pure magenta #FF00FF at every pixel outside the object AND at every
pixel visible through the opening, with no shadow, no floor, no gradient, no vignette. The object
must be cleanly separable from the background.

Constraints: one object only. Absolutely no text, lettering, numerals, labels, arrows, measurement
marks, captions, borders, or watermarks anywhere in the image. No second object, no scenery, no
figures, no ground, no wall behind it.
